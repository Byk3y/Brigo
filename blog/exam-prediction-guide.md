## 🚀 How the Magic Happens

Brigo doesn't just "guess." Our AI analyzes your study materials using a sophisticated multi-source engine. It looks at your content from two different angles:

1.  **Trend Analysis**: If you upload past exam papers, Brigo identifies recurring patterns, identifying topics that are "High Frequency" or "Rising Trends."
2.  **Concept Density**: By scanning your lecture notes and textbooks, Brigo identifies core concepts that are foundational to the subject—even if they haven't appeared on a past paper yet.

The result? A prioritized list of questions that match the style, difficulty level, and format of your specific exam.

---

## 💎 The Pro Secret: Power Up with Past Papers

If you want the most accurate predictions, **Past Questions are your "Secret Sauce."**

While Brigo can predict questions from just a textbook, adding past exam papers (PDFs or images) changes the game. When you add past questions to your notebook:

*   **Pattern Recognition**: Brigo sees *how* your examiners ask questions. Are they multiple-choice heavy? Do they love 20-mark essays on cell biology?
*   **Sequential Numbering**: Brigo tracks trends over years. If a topic has appeared every year for the last three years, Brigo knows it's a high-probability target.
*   **Model Marking Schemes**: For essay-style predictions, Brigo doesn't just give you an answer; it provides a **Model Marking Scheme**. It shows you exactly how points are awarded (e.g., "1. Define term (2 marks), 2. Explain process (3 marks)") so you can practice like a pro.

## 📝 Don't Forget Your Notes!

Past papers tell us *how* they ask, but your **Lecture Notes** tell us *what* to ask. 

Your notes contain the specific emphasis your professor or teacher has placed on certain topics. By combining notes with past papers, Brigo creates a "hybrid" prediction that is tailored not just to the subject, but to **your specific course**.

---

## 📊 Understanding Your Results

When you generate a prediction in Brigo, your questions are grouped into three levels:

*   🔴 **High Priority**: These are your "Non-Negotiables." High likelihood of appearing based on recurring patterns and concept importance.
*   🟡 **Medium Priority**: Solid topics that frequently support core concepts. Worth reviewing thoroughly.
*   🟢 **Lower Priority**: Good for "safety" coverage once you've mastered the higher priorities.

---

## 💡 3 Tips for 100% Accuracy

1.  **Consistency is Key**: Upload at least **3 to 5 years** of past exam papers if you can find them. The more data Brigo has, the better it can spot the trends.
2.  **Mix Your Sources**: Don't just upload the textbook. Add your class slides, handwritten notes (yes, we can read them!), and past papers together in one notebook.
3.  **Read the "Reasoning"**: Every predicted question comes with a "Reasoning" section. Read it! It will tell you *why* Brigo thinks that topic is important (e.g., "Predicted due to high recurrence in 2023-2024 papers").
