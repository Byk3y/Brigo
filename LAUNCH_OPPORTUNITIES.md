# Brigo App Launch Opportunities

Comprehensive list of platforms, communities, and blogs to launch your educational productivity app.

## 🚀 Primary Launch Platforms
These are the high-traffic general platforms to get your first wave of users.

- **[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/)**: The gold standard. Plan your launch day carefully.
- **[Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/)**: Great community for feedback and building in public.
- **[BetaList](https://betalist.com/)**: excellent for pre-launch/beta access if you aren't fully public yet.
- **[Show HN (Hacker News)](https://news.ycombinator.com/show)**: strict rules, but massive technical audience if you can frame the "how it works" interestingly.
- **[Launched!](https://launched.io/)**: Community for showcasing new products.
- **[Microlaunch](https://microlaunch.net/)**: A newer alternative to Product Hunt with a longer exposure window.
- **[Startup Stash](https://startupstash.com/)**: Directory of tools and resources, good for listing.

## 🎓 Education & Student Niche
Since Brigo is student-focused, these specific channels will yield higher conversion quality.

### Directories & Communities
- **[EdSurge](https://www.edsurge.com/)**: The leading news/resource site for edtech. Look for "EdTech Index" or product submission.
- **[eLearning Industry](https://elearningindustry.com/)**: Massive platform for edtech reviews and articles.
- **[Educational App Store](https://www.educationalappstore.com/)**: Dedicated review site for educational apps (certified by teachers).
- **[Common Sense Education](https://www.commonsense.org/education/)**: Highly trusted reviews. Submit your app for evaluation.
- **[Teachers with Apps](https://www.teacherswithapps.com/)**: Reviews of edtech tools.

### Student/Academic Blogs to Pitch
*Pitch angle: "The ultimate student productivity notebook"*
- **[Edutopia](https://www.edutopia.org/)**: Focuses on K-12, but widely read by educators who recommend tools.
- **[TeachThought](https://www.teachthought.com/)**: Features "Best Apps" lists regularly.
- **[The Learning Network (NYT)](https://www.nytimes.com/spotlight/learning-network)**: High tier, but occasionally features tools.
- **[Study Web Development](https://studywebdevelopment.com/student-blogs.html)**: Often lists resources for devs/students.

## ⚡ Productivity & Tech Newsletters
Get featured in "App of the Week" sections.

- **[Wonder Tools](https://wondertools.substack.com/)**: High-quality newsletter about digital tools.
- **[Dense Discovery](https://www.densediscovery.com/)**: carefully curated productivity apps.
- **[ToolFinder](https://www.toolfinder.co/)**: Directory and newsletter for productivity tools.
- **[Keep Productive](https://www.youtube.com/user/KeepProductive)**: (YouTube/Newsletter) The authority on productivity software.

## 📱 App Store Optimization (ASO) & Submission
You found some guides on 2025 submission, don't forget:
- **Apple's "App of the Day"**: You can actually [pitch directly to the App Store editors](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/promote/). This is the highest leverage move if accepted.
- **AppAdvice**: A long-running site for "Apps Gone Free" and reviews.
